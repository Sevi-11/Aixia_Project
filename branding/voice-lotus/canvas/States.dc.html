Six voice states

Each one animates live. The silhouette never breaks — only the splay angle, the seed and the water change.

pivot 100,148
ease cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)
Idle
CALL OPEN · NOBODY TALKING
Whole bloom breathes at 4.2s, roughly a resting breath. Seed fades with it. Nothing splays — the flower is closed and waiting.
Listening
MIC LIVE · VAD ACTIVE
Petals splay open and hold with a slow sway. Ripples travel outward across the water line — the one state where the floor moves.
Thinking
RETRIEVAL → GENERATION
Light sweeps petal to petal, outer to centre, while the bloom sways ±2.6°. Covers the RAG latency without borrowing a spinner.
Speaking
TTS PLAYING · AMPLITUDE BOUND
Splay and scale ride the output level. Offset phases keep it from pumping in unison. The seed beats on every syllable.
Trouble
NO MIC · NETWORK · MODEL ERROR
Bloom contracts on a slow 1.7s pulse, colour drops to clay, water flattens. Always paired with text — the mark alone never carries the reason.
Muted
MIC OFF · SESSION ALIVE
Petals fold inward, colour drains to slate, the seed goes hollow and everything stops. The slash lives on the mute button, never across the mark.
